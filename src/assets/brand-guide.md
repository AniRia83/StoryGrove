## Design Rules

- Soft rounded corners
- Botanical illustrations
- Warm paper backgrounds
- Gentle shadows
- Subtle animations
- Woodland-inspired icons

Avoid:

- Neon colors
- Harsh shadows
- Sharp corners
- Clutter
- Cartoon style
